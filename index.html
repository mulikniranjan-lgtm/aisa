About

Vision, mission & focus areas.

The department aims to create AI professionals who can design responsible solutions, solve real-world problems, and build scalable intelligent systems.

Vision

  • To become a centre of excellence in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science education.
  • To produce globally competent and socially responsible AI engineers.

Mission

  • Impart strong foundations in mathematics, programming and AI techniques.
  • Promote project-based learning, innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Encourage industry collaboration for internships and live projects.
  • Ensure ethical and responsible use of AI technologies.

Program Outcomes

  • Apply AI & DS concepts to build intelligent applications.
  • Design and analyse algorithms and data pipelines.
  • Work effectively in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Communicate technical ideas clearly and professionally.
  • Adapt to new tools, frameworks and technologies.
